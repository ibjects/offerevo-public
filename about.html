About OfferEvo

Local places. Customer ideas.

OfferEvo is a free local deals platform where customers suggest offers, owners approve them, and people discover, share and redeem them in store.

Updated 2 October 2026

Why we built OfferEvo

A favourite café, barber or neighbourhood shop can mean a lot to its regulars. OfferEvo gives those customers a way to suggest a deal that gives someone else a reason to visit. The venue stays in control: every suggestion needs its approval before publication. We build OfferEvo in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, for local places and the people who support them.

How the platform works

Anyone can browse public offers and profiles. Sign in with Google to save, create or redeem a deal. Owners add their venues to the same account and use the app to review submissions, choose which offers go live and check redemption codes. Each deal has its own conditions, duration and redemption limits. Approval starts its duration; a QR or eight-character code is checked in store when someone uses it.

  • Free for customers and owners.
  • Works in a browser on phones and computers.
  • One account can manage multiple venues.
  • One redemption per person for each offer.

Who it is for

OfferEvo is for customers who enjoy discovering and sharing local deals, and for cafés, restaurants, salons, barbers, gyms, shops and local services that want to review customer promotion ideas. Availability near you depends on which venues have joined. OfferEvo is in public beta; see the help centre for the current product rules.
