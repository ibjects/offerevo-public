Approval and rejection

What happens after you submit an offer.

11 questions

What happens after I submit an offer?

It goes to the business as pending. It is not visible to anyone else until the business approves it.

Link to this answer
How long does approval take?

It depends entirely on the business. OfferEvo does not set a time limit on reviewing an offer.

Link to this answer
How do I know if my offer was approved?

Open My Deals. Approved offers move to the Approved tab and become visible in the feed.

Link to this answer
Why was my offer rejected?

The business decides. If they left a reason it is shown on the offer in the Rejected tab of My Deals.

Link to this answer
Can I appeal a rejection?

There is no appeal process in the app. You can create a different offer for the same business.

Link to this answer
Can a business change an offer instead of rejecting it?

No. A business can approve or reject an offer, not edit it.

Link to this answer
Can an offer go live without the business approving it?

No. Approval by the business is the only way an offer becomes visible.

Link to this answer
Can a business un-approve an offer that is live?

Not directly. A business can archive itself, which pauses all of its offers.

Link to this answer
Does a rejected offer stay in my account?

Yes, under the Rejected tab in My Deals, with the reason if one was given.

Link to this answer
Can I resubmit a rejected offer?

Not the same one. Create a new offer instead.

Link to this answer
Who sees my offer while it is pending?

You and the business owner. It does not appear in the feed, in search or on your public profile.
