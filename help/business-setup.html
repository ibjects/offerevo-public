Setting up a business

Adding your business and setting its rules.

19 questions

How do I add my business?

Sign in, open Business, and add your business. Your account gains the business tools immediately.

Link to this answer
Do I need a separate business account?

No. A business is added to the account you already have.

Link to this answer
Can I have more than one business?

Yes. You can add several and switch between them.

Link to this answer
What details does a business need?

A name, a category, and an address with country, state and city. A logo is optional.

Link to this answer
Why does my business need a category?

The category is what people filter and search by, and every offer for your business inherits it.

Link to this answer
My category is not in the list. What do I do?

You can propose a new one. It is visible to you straight away and to everyone once approved.

Link to this answer
What is the redemption cap?

The most times any single offer for your business can be redeemed.

Link to this answer
What is the difference between fixed and distributed?

Fixed means every offer uses exactly your number. Distributed lets the person creating the offer choose from a range up to your cap.

Link to this answer
What range does distributed give?

Steps at roughly 10, 20, 40, 60 and 100 percent of your cap. A cap of 50 offers 5, 10, 20, 30 and 50.

Link to this answer
What is the maximum offer length?

The most days any offer for your business can run, with the same fixed or distributed choice as redemptions.

Link to this answer
If I lower my cap, does it shrink offers that are already live?

No. Each offer stores the numbers it was approved with, so live offers are never changed by a later settings change.

Link to this answer
What is the brand colour?

The colour used on your offer cards. You can lock it so every offer uses it.

Link to this answer
What happens if I lock my colour?

People creating offers for you cannot choose a colour, and every offer uses your brand colour.

Link to this answer
Can I use any colour as my brand colour?

Yes. Enter any hex value or use the colour picker. If a colour would be unreadable in light or dark mode, it is adjusted for that mode only.

Link to this answer
Why does the colour look different in dark mode?

So it stays readable. Your exact colour is kept in whichever mode it already works in, and only the other mode is adjusted.

Link to this answer
Does my business have a public page?

Yes, listing its active offers. Anyone can open it without an account.

Link to this answer
Where do I change my business details?

Business, then Settings on the business you want to change.

Link to this answer
If I rename my business, what happens to its offers?

They keep working and show the new name.

Link to this answer
If I change my category, what happens to live offers?

They move to the new category, because the category comes from the business rather than the offer.
