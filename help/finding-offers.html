Finding offers

Browsing, searching, location and saving.

16 questions

How do I find offers near me?

The feed shows live offers for your city. Open OfferEvo and they are the first thing you see.

Link to this answer
How does OfferEvo know where I am?

It uses your location if you have already allowed it, otherwise the city on your profile, otherwise an approximate city from your internet connection.

Link to this answer
Will OfferEvo ask for my location?

Not on its own. The feed never triggers a location prompt unless you tap the location button yourself.

Link to this answer
The feed shows the wrong city. How do I fix it?

Tap the location chip at the top of the feed to use your device location, or set your city on your profile.

Link to this answer
Can I see offers in another city?

Yes. Clear the location filter to see offers from everywhere.

Link to this answer
How do I search?

Use the search button on the feed. Search matches the offer text, the business name and the category.

Link to this answer
Why does the feed show different offers each time?

The first page is shuffled so the same offers are not always on top.

Link to this answer
How do I filter by category?

Use the category chips above the feed. Your choice is remembered for next time.

Link to this answer
What does the number on an offer card mean?

It shows how many redemptions are left and how many days the offer still runs.

Link to this answer
How do I save an offer?

Tap the bookmark on the card. Saved offers are under Saved.

Link to this answer
Do I need an account to save an offer?

Yes. Tapping the bookmark while signed out takes you to sign in.

Link to this answer
Why has a saved offer disappeared?

It was archived by the person who created it, or the business archived itself. Saved shows a note counting offers that are no longer available.

Link to this answer
Do expired offers stay in Saved?

Yes, in a separate Expired section, so you can still see what you had saved.

Link to this answer
What does sold out mean on a card?

The offer has been redeemed the maximum number of times it allowed.

Link to this answer
Can I see all offers from one business?

Yes. Open the business's public page to see its active offers.

Link to this answer
Can I see all offers from one person?

Yes. Tap their handle on any card to open their public profile.
