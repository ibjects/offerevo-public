Getting started

Accounts, signing in, and installing OfferEvo.

14 questions

What is OfferEvo?

OfferEvo is a local deals platform where customers create offers for businesses they like. The business approves each offer before it goes live, and then anyone can find, share and use it.

Link to this answer
Do I need an account to browse offers?

No. You can browse the feed, open any offer and view public profiles without signing in. You need an account to save, create or redeem an offer.

Link to this answer
How do I create an account?

Open app.offerevo.com and sign in with Google. Your account is created the first time you sign in.

Link to this answer
Can I sign up with an email and password?

Not currently. Google is the only sign-in method.

Link to this answer
Is OfferEvo free?

Yes, for both customers and business owners.

Link to this answer
Do I need to download an app?

No. OfferEvo runs in your browser. You can also install it to your home screen, which makes it behave like an app.

Link to this answer
How do I install OfferEvo on my phone?

Open app.offerevo.com in your browser and choose Add to Home Screen. On Android an install prompt usually appears on its own.

Link to this answer
Does OfferEvo work on a computer?

Yes. Everything works on desktop, though redeeming is designed for a phone since you show a code in store.

Link to this answer
Do I need one account for both customer and business use?

One account is enough. Adding a business to your existing account gives you the business tools alongside everything else.

Link to this answer
Is there a separate business app?

No. The business tools appear in the same app once you add a business.

Link to this answer
Which browsers are supported?

Any current browser. Some features, such as sharing an image straight to another app, depend on what your browser supports.

Link to this answer
Why does sharing only offer to download the image on my computer?

Desktop browsers do not accept image sharing, so OfferEvo saves the file and copies the caption instead. On a phone the normal share sheet opens.

Link to this answer
Is OfferEvo available in my country?

OfferEvo is open to anyone. What you see depends on whether businesses near you have joined.

Link to this answer
What does it cost a business to join?

Nothing. Adding a business and approving offers is free.
