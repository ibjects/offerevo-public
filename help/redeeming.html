Redeeming an offer

Codes, QR, and using a deal in store.

18 questions

How do I redeem an offer?

Open the offer, tap Redeem, and show the QR code or the eight-character code to staff at the business.

Link to this answer
What is the eight-character code?

It is four characters identifying the offer plus four identifying you, shown with a dash between them for readability.

Link to this answer
Do I need an account to redeem?

Yes. The code includes a part that identifies you, so you have to be signed in.

Link to this answer
Does the code work if I have no signal in the shop?

The business needs a connection to confirm the code, so redeeming requires one of you to be online.

Link to this answer
How does the business confirm my code?

They scan your QR code with their camera, or type the eight characters in. Your screen turns green the moment it goes through.

Link to this answer
Can I redeem the same offer twice?

No. Each person can redeem a given offer once.

Link to this answer
Can I redeem several different offers at the same business?

Yes, as long as each one is a different offer.

Link to this answer
What does already redeemed mean?

That offer has already been used on your account and cannot be used again.

Link to this answer
What does sold out mean when redeeming?

The offer reached its maximum number of redemptions before you got there.

Link to this answer
What does that offer has ended mean?

The offer passed its expiry date and can no longer be redeemed.

Link to this answer
What does that offer belongs to another business mean?

The code was entered at a business that does not own this offer. Codes only work at the business the offer is for.

The code did not match any offer. Why?

The code was typed incorrectly, or the offer no longer exists. Check the characters and try again.

Link to this answer
Does my code change?

No. Your half of the code is permanent, and each offer's half is fixed too.

Link to this answer
Is it safe if someone sees my code?

Only the business the offer belongs to can accept it, and only once. It is not a payment method.

Can I redeem on behalf of a friend?

No. The code identifies your account, so each person redeems with their own.

Do I have to buy something to redeem?

That depends on the offer's conditions. Read them on the offer page before you go.

Where do I see what I have redeemed?

My Deals has a Redeemed tab listing every offer you have used and when.

Can the business refuse my code?

The conditions on the offer are what it is subject to. If something is unclear, ask the business before you order.
