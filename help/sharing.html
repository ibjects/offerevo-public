Sharing

Links, images and posting to social apps.

12 questions

How do I share an offer?

Tap the share button on any offer card or on the offer page. You can share a link or an image.

Link to this answer
What does the shared image look like?

It is a card with the offer, the business, and a short link, in the offer's colour.

Link to this answer
What sizes can I share?

Square for feed posts, Story for full-screen stories, and Wide for link previews and desktop chat. Square is the default.

Link to this answer
Can I post an offer straight to my Instagram story?

Yes, on a phone. Choose Story, tap Share, and pick Instagram from your phone's share sheet.

Link to this answer
Which apps can I share to?

Whatever is installed on your phone. OfferEvo hands the image to your phone's own share sheet, so the list is yours, not ours.

Link to this answer
Why can I not share directly to Instagram on my computer?

Desktop browsers do not support sharing files to other apps. The image downloads and the caption is copied instead.

Link to this answer
Does the shared image include my save and share buttons?

No. The image is a clean card with no buttons on it.

Link to this answer
What is in the caption?

The offer, the business name, and the link to the offer, with the link at the end so it stays tappable.

Link to this answer
Where does a shared link go?

To the offer's own page, which anyone can open without an account.

Link to this answer
Does a shared link open the app if I have it installed?

On Android, yes. On iPhone a link opens in Safari, which is a limitation of the platform.

Link to this answer
Can I share an expired offer?

The link still opens, and the page shows that the offer has ended.

Link to this answer
Do I get credit when someone uses an offer I shared?

Offers you created list you as their creator. Sharing an offer someone else created is not tracked.
