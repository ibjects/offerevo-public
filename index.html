Questions, answered

Before you try OfferEvo.

What does OfferEvo do?

Customers create discounts and deals for local businesses. Businesses approve them before they go live. People can then discover, share and use those offers.

Can an offer go live without business approval?

No. Customers can create offers for your business, but each offer must be approved by the business before it goes live.

How do I use an offer?

Check its terms, visit the business and show your QR code or eight-character code. Staff check the code to confirm the offer.

Can I use an offer someone else created?

Yes. You can find offers on OfferEvo or through someone sharing them. You do not need to know the creator. Just follow the offer’s terms.

How do I get started?

Create an account at app.offerevo.com. You can browse offers or submit your own. If you own a business, add it to the same account. No app download is needed.

Is OfferEvo free?

Yes. OfferEvo is free for customers and business owners.

Have more questions?

Our help centre has detailed answers on creating offers, approval, redeeming codes and running a business. You can also message us or email hello@offerevo.com.
